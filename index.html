Runtao XUE

I am currently pursuing a master’s degree in Mathematical Engineering and Biostatistics at Université Paris Cité, following a dual bachelor’s degree (Bi-licence) in Mathematics and Computer Science.

My interdisciplinary academic training has given me a solid foundation in mathematics and computer science. I place particular emphasis on logical reasoning, spatial thinking and abstract modelling, approaching complex problems from different perspectives to explore possible solutions. I am comfortable conducting research and development independently and also have experience in teamwork, project organisation and coordination.

My research interests and practical experience span several interdisciplinary fields, including biomedical data analysis, software engineering, embodied AI, AI model training and AI-powered visual creation. I enjoy exploring connections between disciplines and technologies, and seek to turn theoretical knowledge into research outcomes and creative work with practical value.
